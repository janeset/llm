# Design inspiration: top 50 from Awwwards

Researched on Awwwards on 2026-10-21 for a design upgrade of AI / LLM Prep. I searched Awwwards by
keyword (learning, course, academy, coding, developer, AI, agent, prompt, quiz, dashboard…) and by brand
(dev tools, AI labs, edtech), went through every Site of the Day since mid-2023, and read the award page of
about 140 candidates. These 50 are the ones with something we can actually use: a pattern for teaching,
for developer tools, or for showing data and careers. Every link responded when checked on 2026-10-21.

Awards: **SOTM** Site of the Month, **SOTD** Site of the Day (score out of 10), **HM** Honorable Mention.

## Open these 10 first

1. Why Zero (#1): the closest match to us, AI-native learning with a game-like roadmap
2. Learning Music by Ableton (#2): still the best "learn by doing in the browser" lesson format
3. The Shape of Intelligence (#3): an AI history you can run experiments in
4. The Creative Website Manual (#7): a long technical lesson that stays easy to read
5. Linea Prompt (#21): a ⌘K command palette done well
6. Stripe Dot Dev (#23): themes and a real console on a developer site
7. Heron AI (#25): suggest, then approve, then apply. A model for hints and "Debug this"
8. Mathical (#26): streaming AI output and dashboards that update as you type
9. AI in Design Report 2026 (#41): research turned into an editorial data story
10. GFI Career Pathways (#46): career switchers, job archetypes, skills. Our Roles page, done beautifully

## What keeps coming up, and where it fits

- **Lessons you can play with.** A live widget sits next to short text (#2, #3, #13, #14, #18). We already run Python in the browser; the upgrade is putting the runner beside the explanation instead of below it.
- **Progress you earn.** Unlocks, timelines and a celebration at the end (#1, #12, #45). Fits the 10-week roadmap and week gates.
- **Keyboard-first developer UX.** A command palette, a terminal and a console (#21, #22, #23). Fits Ctrl+K search and the developer-toolkit lesson.
- **Hints that suggest, then reveal.** Suggest → approve → apply, plus streaming output (#24, #25, #26). Fits hints, model answers and Debug this.
- **Bento dashboards and scores.** (#26, #29, #30). Fits Home stats, streaks and role readiness.
- **Research as an editorial story.** (#41–#44, #46). Fits Roles (skill demand) and the curriculum research.
